Consent

Building, Losing, and Recovering Trust in Human–Robot Interaction

Please read this consent information before deciding whether to participate. Separate study and task instructions follow.

Purpose

This study explores people’s experiences and decisions when working with a robot assistant.

What you will do

Locate items in a storage rack, consider the robot’s recommendations, and make your own choices. You will also answer brief questions about your experience.

Data collected

We collect choices, response times, Manual Check use, questionnaire answers, and demographics. Data collected will be anonymized.

Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time by closing this page. Demographic questions require a response; where offered, you may select Prefer not to say. Written feedback may be left blank.

Risks and benefits

No risks beyond those of ordinary computer use are expected. There are no direct benefits to you from participating. If you feel uncomfortable at any point, you may stop by closing this browser tab using the X on the tab.

Privacy

Data collected will be anonymized. Do not enter identifying information in written answers. Responses are sent when you press Submit on the final thank-you screen. Closing or refreshing before submission loses your responses.

Questions? Contact Steven Anyansi: sanyansi@purdue.edu or Panagiotis (Peter) Papadopoulos: papadop@purdue.edu.

If you do not wish to participate, close this page.
