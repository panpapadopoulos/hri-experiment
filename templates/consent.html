Study information and consent

Building, Losing, and Recovering Trust in Human–Robot Interaction

Please read the information below before deciding whether to participate.

Purpose

This study explores people’s experiences and decisions when working with a robot assistant.

What you will do

Locate items in a storage rack, consider the robot’s recommendations, and make your own choices. You will also answer brief questions about your experience.

Data collected

Non-identifiable behavioral data will be collected, including choices, response times, and use of Manual Check. We also collect questionnaire responses and demographic information.

Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time by closing this page. Demographic questions require a response; where offered, you may select Prefer not to say. Written feedback may be left blank.

Risks or discomfort

Timed decisions may cause mild pressure, fatigue, or frustration. You may stop if you feel uncomfortable.

Privacy

Do not enter your name or other identifying information in written answers. Responses are stored with a study or platform participant identifier; they are not guaranteed to be anonymous.

If you do not wish to participate, close this page.
